About us

Every language deserves a future.

Our vision

A world where no language is forgotten, kept alive by many small actions from many people.

Our mission

Language preservation usually needs experts and years of work. We break it into bite-sized tasks, so anyone can make a lasting cultural impact in five minutes.

How it works

  1. Learn. Pick a region on the map and learn phrases through short, game-like lessons.
  2. Contribute. Verify a translation or record a phrase. Learners and contributors are the same people.
  3. Verify. Volunteers review each other's work. Teachers and local elders give the final approval.

Who owns the recordings?

The speakers and their communities. Every recording comes with consent, and speakers can withdraw it at any time.
